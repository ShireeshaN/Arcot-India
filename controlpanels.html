SUBSTATION & CONTROL PANELS

Protection • Automation • Synchronization • LT Systems

SUBSTATION CONTROL & PROTECTION PANELS

Protection Systems

Overcurrent, Earth Fault, Differential & Distance Protection.
Feeder (11kV / 33kV) Control, Trip & Alarm Integration.
Transformer Protection with Buchholz Relay, WTI/OTI Monitoring & Alarm Annunciation.

CONTROL & RELAY (C&R) PANELS

Substation Control

Breaker Control, Indication Lamps, Trip/Close Push Buttons, Mimic Diagram & Annunciator System. Suitable for 33/11kV & 66/11kV Substations.

SCADA & AUTOMATION PANELS

Smart Monitoring

RTU Panels, SCADA Control Panels, Remote Monitoring Systems & Communication Panels for modern automated substations.

SYNCHRONIZING PANELS

Generator & Power Plant Use

Generator Synchronization, Load Sharing Control & Automatic Synchronization Systems for industrial and power plant applications.

LT CONTROL PANELS

Motor & Pump Systems

DOL, Star-Delta, Soft Starter & VFD Panels. Pump Control Panels for Water Supply & Irrigation Projects.

    Frequently
    Asked Questions
    What types of control panels do you offer?
    We offer a wide range of panels including PCC, MCC, PLC, APFC, VFD, LT panels, HT panels, and customized control panels as per project requirements.
    Can you provide customized control panel solutions?
    Absolutely. We design and build customized control panels based on load requirements, application needs, site conditions, and client specifications.
    What quality checks are performed on control panels?
    Each panel undergoes rigorous testing including insulation resistance, continuity, functional testing, and safety checks before delivery and installation.
    Do you handle installation and commissioning?
    Yes. We provide complete services including installation, testing, commissioning, and on-site support for all control panels.
