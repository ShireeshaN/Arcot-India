Keep your lights looking their best and working efficiently by following these simple care tips:

  • Always switch off and unplug before cleaning.
  • Clean with a soft, dry, or lightly damp cloth; avoid harsh chemicals or scrubbing.
  • Do not expose the fixtures to water or high moisture.
  • Inspect periodically for loose components or signs of wear.
  • Avoid touching bulbs directly with bare hands to prolong their lifespan.
  • Ensure adequate airflow around the fixtures to prevent overheating.
  • Handle carefully during installation or relocation to avoid damage.
