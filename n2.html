Vehicle Incident Detection System (VIDS)

AI-powered adaptive street lighting that adjusts brightness automatically based on movement and environmental conditions for energy efficiency.

  • Real-time detection of accidents
  • collisions, and stopped vehicles
  • AI classifies severity based on impact and traffic density
  • Immediate ICCC alert with geo-coordinates and timestamps
  • Supports video evidence with pre/post-incident footage
  • Enables predictive analytics for accident-prone zones
  • Automatically triggers nearby Smart Pole emergency lights
  • Sends instant SOS notification to emergency services
  • Uses AI behaviour modelling to minimize false alerts
  • Works in all lighting and weather conditions
  • Reduces emergency response time dramatically
