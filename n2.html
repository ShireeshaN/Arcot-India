Intelligent Traffic Management System (ITMS)

Accident Detection

The AI-driven system provides real-time identification of accidents and stopped vehicles, classifying severity and instantly alerting ICCC with geo-coordinates and footage—reducing emergency response time dramatically.

Speed Violation Detection

Intelligent radar identifies overspeeding vehicles with lane-level precision, captures photo and video evidence, integrates with ANPR, and auto-generates e-Challans — ensuring safer and more disciplined roads.

Radar Speed Measurement

Advanced Doppler sensors monitor vehicle speed with ±2% accuracy, feeding live data to ICCC dashboards, displaying alerts, and supporting Vahan integration for automated enforcement and analytics.
