High-Voltage Transmission Lines

Engineered to transport electricity over vast distances, HV transmission lines handle voltages from 69 kV up to 765 kV and beyond. Their design varies by type—overhead, underground, and submarine—ensuring efficient, reliable, and safe power delivery to industries, cities, and communities

Transformers, Insulators & Breakers

From shaping voltage levels to safeguarding against faults, transformers, insulators, and breakers form the backbone of modern power systems. Together, they ensure safe, efficient, and reliable electricity across the grid.

From Air-Insulated (AIS) to Gas-Insulated (GIS), and step-up to step-down, substations transform voltage levels and route electricity seamlessly. They stand as the critical hubs that balance, protect, and control the grid.
