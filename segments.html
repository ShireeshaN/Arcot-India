Applications

Power Systems

We provide reliable power infrastructure covering generation, transmission, and distribution, along with advanced LT and HT panel boards for efficient and safe operations.

Energy Solutions

Our energy portfolio includes solar, wind, biomass, and hydel power projects, delivering sustainable and future-ready solutions for diverse energy needs.

Lighting Technology

From homes to stadiums, we design advanced lighting systems that enhance safety, efficiency, and aesthetics across residential, commercial, industrial, and public spaces.

Automation Systems

We deliver intelligent automation for homes, industries, hospitals, hotels, and commercial spaces, powered by IoT for smarter and more efficient environments.

Smart City Solutions

Our smart city innovations such as smart poles integrate lighting, connectivity, and safety features, creating sustainable and connected urban spaces.

Innovation & R&D

Driven by innovation, our R&D division develops next-generation products including smart poles, bollards, conference tables, bar counters, and smart window leaf AC systems.
