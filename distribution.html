Frequently
Asked Questions
What types of power distribution systems do you handle?
We handle LT, HT, and EHV distribution systems, including overhead lines, underground cabling, substations, and switchgear installations.
Do you provide turnkey power distribution solutions?
Yes. We offer end-to-end solutions including design, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning, and ongoing maintenance.
Can you upgrade or modernize existing distribution networks?
Absolutely. We specialize in system upgrades, load balancing, loss reduction, and capacity enhancements to improve efficiency and reliability.
How do you handle power outages and faults?
We use systematic fault detection, rapid response teams, and preventive maintenance practices to minimize downtime and restore power quickly.
