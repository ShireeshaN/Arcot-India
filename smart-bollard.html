SMART BOLLARD

Arcot Smart Bollard

Hybrid elegance redefined — where connectivity meets craftsmanship.

Bluetooth Connectivity

Seamless pairing for instant smart control and synchronized lighting.

Wi-Fi Integration

Connects to the cloud for diagnostics, updates, and automated scheduling.

Wireless Charging

Power your devices effortlessly with built-in wireless charging zones.

Audio System

Immersive ambient sound — designed for parks, walkways, and plazas.

Tuning & Dimming

Precision control of brightness and tone to set any environment’s mood.

Smart Cities & Urban Infrastructure

Smart Bollards play a critical role in modern smart cities by combining lighting, connectivity, security, and data intelligence into a single street element. They enhance urban aesthetics while supporting IoT-based monitoring, public safety, and energy efficiency across city landscapes.
