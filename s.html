SPOTLIGHT GARDEN

Create effectual garden lighting with spotlights

Our energy-efficient LED floodlights in IP65 design are perfectly suited to light bushes and trees, for example. You also use lighting to create different spaces in the garden or to illuminate the facade.

Our floodlights can be mounted on walls, ceilings or ground, in private or public environments. Available in different sizes and design with different light distribution. LED floodlights are developed and produced to withstand our Nordic climate. In addition, LED spotlights are easy to install and require minimal maintenance, making them a practical choice for enhancing the aesthetics of your outdoor lighting.
