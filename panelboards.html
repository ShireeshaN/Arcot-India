Frequently
Asked Questions
What types of panel boards do you provide?
We provide LT panel boards, HT panel boards, distribution boards, feeder panels, and customized panel boards as per application requirements.
Can panel boards be customized for specific applications?
Absolutely. Panel boards can be customized based on load capacity, number of feeders, protection requirements, and site conditions.
Do you provide installation and commissioning services?
Yes. We offer complete installation, testing, and commissioning services to ensure proper functioning of panel boards.
Do you provide maintenance and after-sales support?
Yes. We offer preventive maintenance, breakdown support, and AMC services to ensure long-term performance and reliability.
